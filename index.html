Recycling Guidance by Core Material

♻️

Plastic

Best Practices: Rinse before recycling. Remove caps. Avoid plastic bags. Check for recycling symbols #1-#7.

What NOT to recycle: Plastic bags, foam, food containers with grease.

📄

Paper & Pulp

Best Practices: Keep dry. Bundle newspapers. Remove plastic windows from envelopes. Flatten boxes.

What NOT to recycle: Paper with food residue, wax coated paper, tissues.

📦

Cardboard

Best Practices: Break down boxes. Remove tape. Keep dry. Remove any non-cardboard materials.

What NOT to recycle: Wet or greasy cardboard, waxed cardboard, cardboard with styrofoam.
